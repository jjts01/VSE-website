Launch

Product launches built for the wide shot.

The launch is the one event where the picture is the message. Studio design, lighting, LED and graphics that make the reveal land, and a stream that reaches everyone at once.

A launch event is judged on its wide shot. The set, the lighting and the graphics have to make the product (and the people presenting it) look like the most important thing happening that day. Then the reveal has to be flawless, which is why the moments that cannot fail are pre-recorded to cinematic standard and the moments that benefit from being live. The Q&A, the reactions, the first demo questions, are live.

We design launch productions in our studio or a venue: built sets, LED walls where the budget warrants them, a graphics package designed for the broadcast, multi-camera coverage, and simulcast delivery to your website, YouTube and LinkedIn simultaneously so the audience is wherever it wants to be. Clips for social are cut during the show and ready when it ends.

Common questions

Launch event questions

Should the launch be live or pre-recorded?

Usually both: the reveal and demo pre-recorded to a standard live can't match, with live segments for Q&A and reaction. The join is invisible when it's produced in the same set and lighting.

Can you stream to our website and social at once?

Yes. Simulcast to your site player, YouTube and LinkedIn with a restreaming layer, plus an independent standby stream.

Do we need an LED wall?

Not necessarily. A well-lit built set with an LED panel as one element delivers most of the look for much less. The backdrop guide compares the options.

How much does a launch production cost?

Studio-based launches with full production typically start around £8,000+VAT; venue productions with LED and multi-camera scale to £20,000+.
