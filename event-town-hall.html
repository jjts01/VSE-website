Internal comms

Town halls your whole company actually watches.

Internal comms with production values: a studio-quality show into Teams, Zoom or your intranet, with the Q&A and polls that make it a conversation rather than a broadcast.

Most town halls are a webcam and a slide deck. Attendance is mandatory, attention isn't. The difference between a leadership update people endure and one they remember is production: a set that looks considered, sound that's clear on a phone in a warehouse, graphics that reinforce the message, and interaction that proves leadership is listening.

We produce town halls from our Chichester studio, from a pop-up studio in your own building, or with executives joining remotely on managed links. The show goes into whatever your organisation already uses (Teams town halls, Zoom, your intranet player), with captions for accessibility and reporting so you know who watched. National retailers have trusted us with weekly colleague broadcasts to tens of thousands of staff.

What we deliver

What a VSE town hall includes

01

Studio or on-site set

A branded, pre-lit set in our studio or built in your office; presenters coached to camera in a 20-minute session.

02

Produced feed into your tools

Broadcast-quality programme into Teams, Zoom, Vimeo or your intranet, tested on your corporate network in advance.

03

Live interaction

Moderated Q&A with questions on screen, polls with results live, and a moderator working with your comms team.

04

Captions & accessibility

Live captions (human or automated), BSL on request, corrected captions on the on-demand version.

05

Executive support

Teleprompter, confidence monitor, rehearsal, and a floor manager for the leaders on stage.

06

Reporting

Attendance, watch time and engagement against the previous town hall, plus the on-demand numbers.

Common questions

Town hall questions

Can you stream into Microsoft Teams?

Yes. We deliver a produced feed into Teams town halls, live events or meetings, and we test it on your network beforehand.

How much does a produced town hall cost?

A studio-based town hall with full production typically runs £2,000–£5,000+VAT. Regular series attract a reduced rate.

Can our executives present remotely?

Yes, on managed broadcast links with a technical check in advance, appearing in the same branded frame as the studio presenters.

Do you provide the studio?

We have our own studio in Chichester plus a partner network, or we build a pop-up studio at your premises.
