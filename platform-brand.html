Capabilities

What Brand & trust does

01

Full white-label

Custom domain, fonts, colours, layouts, email templates and app icon. No "powered by" unless you want it.

02

Agency reseller mode

Production companies run client events under their own brand with separate workspaces, billing and permissions. A large part of our business.

03

Security

SAML/OIDC SSO, MFA, role-based access, audit logs, encrypted at rest and in transit, penetration tested.

04

Data residency & GDPR

UK/EU hosting, data-processing agreement as standard, consent capture, retention rules, subject-access and deletion tooling.

05

Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA interface, keyboard and screen-reader support, caption controls, BSL picture-in-picture on the stream.

06

Reliability

Redundant streaming, standby destinations, and a human on a channel during your event: not a ticket queue.

07

Content ownership

Your recordings, your data, exported in full whenever you ask. Deleted when you say so.

08

UK/EU data residency, by default

Platform and attendee data hosted on AWS in London and Frankfurt. VSE is a UK-owned company. Your data is not exposed to US CLOUD Act requests via a US parent.

09

Infrastructure certifications & DPA

Built on ISO 27001 / SOC 2-certified AWS infrastructure with VSE's own controls aligned to ISO 27001; data-processing agreement, sub-processor list and security questionnaire answers available on request.

10

Availability & support

99.9% platform availability target, multi-CDN delivery, and 24/7 event-day support with a producer on a channel: not a ticket queue.
