VSE Platform · Engage

Engage: the audience in the show, not just watching it.

A branded participation layer that works on any phone or laptop, in the room or online: moderated Q&A, polls, quizzes with leaderboards, reactions, word clouds, chat — with results rendered into the broadcast graphics by the gallery.

Capabilities

What Engage does

01

Moderated Q&A

Questions submitted from any device, upvoted by the audience, curated by a moderator, pushed to the presenter's screen and rendered on air as a graphic.

02

Polls & quizzes

Multiple choice, ratings, ranking and open text; timed quizzes with live leaderboards; results animated into the stream, not just the app.

03

Reactions & word clouds

Emoji reactions that float across the broadcast, word clouds that build live from audience answers.

04

Live chat & moderation

Hosted chat with profanity filters, slow mode, pre-moderation for public events and message pinning.

05

Captions & translation

Automated or human live captions and real-time translation into the languages your audience chooses, on their own device.

06

In-room & online together

One participation layer for both audiences — the room joins by QR code, online joins from the player. Same polls, same Q&A, fair interleaving.

07

Presenter & moderator views

A presenter tablet with the curated question queue; a moderator console for approving, merging and scheduling; gallery triggers for the graphics.

08

Broadcast-ready outputs

Poll results, questions, word clouds and leaderboards published as graphics sources for vMix, OBS and hardware mixers — so any gallery can put them on air.

09

Scales to 50,000+

Load-tested for town halls with tens of thousands of participants, rate-limited and moderated for public events.
